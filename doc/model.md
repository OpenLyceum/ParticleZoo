# Particle Zoo — model

Physics behind the four screens. Masses in MeV/c², lifetimes in seconds (PDG 2024, rounded).

## Quarks and particles (`src/common/model/`)

- `quarks.ts`: u, d, s, c, b and antiquarks. Charge and baryon number are stored **in thirds** so
  sums stay exact (uud: 2 + 2 − 1 = 3 → Q = +1). Strangeness −1 for s, charm +1 for c, beauty −1 for
  b; antiquarks flip every sign. Constituent masses u 336, d 340, s 486, c 1550, b 4730 MeV.
- **Colour neutrality** with at most three constituents: qqq, q̄q̄q̄ or qq̄.
- **Spin** with no orbital angular momentum: qq̄ → 0 or 1, qqq → ½ or 3/2.
- `particleCatalog.ts`: leptons, γ, W, Z, pseudoscalar and vector mesons, baryon octet and decuplet,
  Λ<sub>c</sub>; antiparticles generated by conjugation. Neutral flavourless mesons list every
  component of their mixture (π⁰ = uū, dd̄), so a uū bag at spin 0 matches π⁰, η and η′ (all shown).
  A test checks every hadron's quark content against its Q, B, S, C and beauty.

## Hadron Builder

Matching uses the multiset of quarks plus the chosen spin. uds at spin ½ is ambiguous (Λ⁰ and Σ⁰
differ only in how the u and d are paired), so both are shown. uuu at spin ½ matches nothing: three
identical quarks in the ground state must have symmetric spins (Δ⁺⁺, 3/2). Colour charges rotate
every 0.9 s in colour-neutral states, to suggest gluon exchange.

## Eightfold Way

Weight diagram: rows are strangeness S, columns are isospin I₃ = Q − (B + S)/2, so lines of constant
charge run diagonally. Decuplet row means: Δ 1232, Σ* 1384.6, Ξ* 1533.4 MeV, steps of 153 and 149 MeV.
Equal spacing predicts ≈ 1684 MeV for S = −3; the Ω⁻ was found at 1672 MeV (Brookhaven, 1964).
A prediction counts as correct within ±30 MeV.

## Confinement

V(r) = σr with σ = 1000 MeV/fm, optionally − (4/3) α<sub>s</sub> ħc / r with α<sub>s</sub> = 0.3.
The string breaks when V reaches the light-pair cost 2 × 340 MeV ≈ 680 MeV: r = 0.68 fm (0.78 fm with
the Coulomb term; solve σr² − Er − k = 0). The new pair alternates uū and dd̄; each end becomes the
spin-0 meson with that content.

## Reaction Court

`reactionRules.ts`, in order:

1. Q, B, L<sub>e</sub>, L<sub>μ</sub> or L<sub>τ</sub> not conserved → forbidden.
2. A decay (one particle before) needs m<sub>before</sub> ≥ Σ m<sub>after</sub>; a collision can
   always be supplied with kinetic energy, so the ledger reports the extra rest energy instead.
3. |ΔS|, |ΔC| or |Δbeauty| ≥ 2 → forbidden (the weak interaction changes flavour one unit at a time).
4. Otherwise allowed by the strongest force whose rules it obeys: neutrinos or any flavour change →
   weak (τ ≳ 10⁻¹⁰ s); photons or charged leptons → electromagnetic (10⁻²⁰–10⁻¹⁶ s); hadrons only →
   strong (~10⁻²³ s).

Spin and angular momentum are not checked (so π⁰ → γ γ γ would pass the ledger); the panel says so.
33 classic reactions are tested.

### Vertex diagrams (`vertexDiagrams.ts`)

Two vertices joined by one boson, with spectator quarks. Each fermion leg is read as "entering the
vertex" (an outgoing particle counts as an incoming antiparticle), so conservation is "everything
entering sums to zero". Rules: γ, Z and g keep flavour (γ needs a charged fermion, g needs quarks);
W changes an up-type quark to a down-type (any generation) or a charged lepton to its own neutrino,
and carries the charge difference. Templates: n β decay, Λ → pπ⁻, π⁺ → μ⁺ν<sub>μ</sub>,
K⁺ → μ⁺ν<sub>μ</sub>, e⁺e⁻ → μ⁺μ⁻ (γ or Z), Δ⁺⁺ → pπ⁺ (gluon).
